The main parts, in plain words

CNC machines

Cut metal blocks into precise parts by following a program. Their spindles spin thousands of times a minute.

Robot arm

Moves parts between machines and the conveyor, exactly the same way every time.

Vision inspection

A camera checks every part for scratches, size and missing features.

Assembly

People and tools put parts together into the finished product.

Machine sensors

Vibration, temperature, power and cycle counts show how each machine is really doing.

Line PLC and MES

The PLC runs the line. The MES records every part, order and stop, and sends data to the cloud.

Engineering office

Engineers design parts in CAD and manage drawings, parts lists and programs in a PLM system.

Digital twin

A live copy of the line on screen, used to see problems and test changes safely.

Cloud AI

Finds patterns across machines, parts and designs, and suggests the fix with the best result.

Six ways the AI helps

Engineering work is full of checks and judgement calls. The AI does the tireless checking so engineers can focus on decisions.

Predict breakdowns

Spots wear early so repairs happen on planned stops, not in the middle of an order.

Find the cause of defects

Links each bad part back to the machine, tool and setting that made it.

Check designs automatically

Compares new drawings with parts lists, programs and past problems before release.

Tune machine settings

Finds the speed and feed that give good parts in the least time.

Balance the line

Moves work between machines so no station holds up the rest.

Test changes on the twin

Tries a new schedule or layout on the digital twin before touching the real line.
