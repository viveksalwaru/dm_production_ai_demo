The parts, in plain words

Sensors

Small devices that measure, all day and night.

  • Air temperature and humidity
  • CO₂ (what plants breathe in)
  • Sunlight
  • Soil moisture in each bed
  • Leaf temperature and wetness
  • Water: plant food (EC) and acidity (pH)
  • Weather outside: wind, rain, sun
Gateway

A small box on a pole that serves all the polyhouses nearby. It collects every reading and sends it over the mobile network. If the internet drops, it saves the readings and sends them later.

Cloud AI

Software that knows what each crop needs at each growth stage. It compares every reading with that and decides what to do, often before a problem shows on the plants.

Machines

They switch on by themselves when told.

  • Fans and wet cooling pad
  • Shade net on the roof
  • Foggers for humidity
  • Drip valves for each bed
  • Dosing pumps for plant food
Your phone

Shows what happened and why, in simple words. You can switch anything to manual, or just ask, "Is my crop OK?"
