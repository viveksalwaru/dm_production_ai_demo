What’s really happening in the industry?

Two big shifts affect freshers and experienced engineers alike.

🤖

AI now writes the code

Work that took hours or days, AI does in seconds — the same work freshers were hired for.

  • Companies spend more on AI tools, less on hiring
  • Work gets done faster, with fewer people
  • Fewer jobs → more people fighting for each one
  • Engineers who use AI well get picked first
